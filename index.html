About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Ajou University, working in the Computer Vision & Machine Learning Lab under the supervision of Prof. Hyung Il Koo. I received my M.S. in Artificial Intelligence and my B.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering, both from Ajou University.

My work spans lightweight multimodal anomaly detection for vehicles (acoustic and CAN signal fusion), real-world computer vision systems such as unauthorized banner detection and RGB–IR image registration, and language-model-based sentence analysis and learning-support systems.
